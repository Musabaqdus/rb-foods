Industrial Provenance

Engineered by BERTUZZI S.r.l. Milan, Italy

Quality in aseptic processing is dictated by thermal kinetics and mechanical shear. R&B Foods invested in genuine Bertuzzi continuous equipment to preserve the delicate aroma, volatile esters, and natural vitamin complex of fresh Pakistani fruit.

01

Three-Tier Washing & Optical Sorting

Immersion sanitization, chlorinated brush spray washing, and continuous manual sorting eliminate substandard fruits before entry.

02

Cold De-Stoning & Turbo Refining

Bertuzzi stainless-steel rotary pulpers gently separate skin and stones without grinding seeds, avoiding bitter polyphenols.

03

Vacuum De-Aeration & Homogenization

Dissolved atmospheric micro-bubbles are extracted under controlled vacuum to prevent oxidative browning and flavor degradation.

04

HTST Tubular Flash Sterilization

High-temperature short-time thermal profile eliminates vegetative cells and spores in seconds, followed by sterile rapid chilling.

05

Cleanroom Aseptic Drum Filling

Automatic sterile filling head hermetically clamps 215kg 4-layer barrier bags inside steel drums with zero recontamination.

Direct Supply from 5,000+ Orchard Acres

R&B Foods operates private estates in Shorkot and maintains direct grower contracts, eliminating speculative middlemen and ensuring harvest-fresh fruit intake.

Automated CIP / SIP Sanitation Cycles

Fully programmable Clean-in-Place and Steam-in-Place systems sterilize all product-contact stainless steel surfaces at 121°C before every processing run.

Continuous Digital SCADA Monitoring

Critical control points (temperature, flow rate, holding time, vacuum pressure) are digitally recorded in real-time for full batch audit compliance.
